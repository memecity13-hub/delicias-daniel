Good to know

Catering questions, answered.

How do I get an exact price?

Call or send the quote form with your date, location, guest count, and food ideas. Delicias Daniel will confirm availability and a custom price.

Can I request a custom menu?

Yes. Include what you have in mind; available dishes and substitutions are confirmed before payment.

How much advance notice is recommended?

At least 72 hours is recommended. Large orders and busy event dates may require additional notice.

Can you accommodate food allergies?

Please disclose allergies before ordering. Food may be prepared in shared areas, so an allergen-free order cannot be guaranteed.

Are delivery and setup included?

No. Pickup pricing is shown on the menu. Delivery, setup, staffing, rentals, and other event services are quoted separately.

Does the form book my event?

No. It prepares a text request. The event is confirmed only after Delicias Daniel accepts the order, both sides agree on the written details, and any required deposit is received.
